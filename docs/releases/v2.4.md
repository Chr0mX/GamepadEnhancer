## GamepadEnhancer v2.4

Requires Windows 10/11, ViGEmBus and the .NET 8 Desktop Runtime. Unzip anywhere you can write to and run `GamepadEnhancer.exe`. Builds on v2.3.

### No rear paddles on PS4
A DualShock 4 has no rear paddles, so with PS4 selected the Rear paddles page and its button are hidden, the controller picture has no rear view or paddle labels, and the dashboard shows "Not on this pad". Switch to Xbox 360 to see them again. Your paddle settings are kept.

### Rear paddles: text and scaling
The controller and rear-paddle pictures are drawn on a fixed canvas that was being squeezed into a short box, shrinking every label to roughly two thirds. The pictures now keep the canvas proportions, so labels stay readable. On the Rear paddles page each paddle label is a larger three-line box: the paddle name, what it does, and a note ("not detected" or the game's name for it).

### Also
